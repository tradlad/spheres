Spheres

At the centre the sun and planets 1 to 3 drone, tuned to each other in just intervals: a planet's speed is its pitch and its orbit together. Gravity pulls the drones into simple ratios; wander and comets pull them out again. Round them, planets 4, 5 and 6 each play an arpeggio, its notes sitting on its circle and lighting as they sound.

Changing things

  • Drag any value: right or up for more. Hold Shift to go finely.
  • Double-click a value to type it: 231 or Bb3 for the root, 3:2 for a speed.
  • With a value chosen, ↑ ↓ step it as the Brick does (Shift for big steps), and Delete puts it back to the start.
  • Everything can be undone: Ctrl Z, and Ctrl Shift Z to redo.

The sky

  • Click a body to choose it: its column lights up and the panel beside the sky says what it is doing.
  • Drag a drone up or down to change its speed. Let go near a simple ratio with the sun and it settles on it; hold Alt to leave it free. Dragging the sun moves the root.
  • Drag an arp planet up or down for shorter or longer notes. Double-click a body to turn it on or off.

The looper

Loop takes the bars before now and as many after, and plays them again and again: the same notes, dice, orbits and moves, not a recording. While it loops only the Sound settings change. Loop again to carry on from there.

Saves and the Brick

The eight slots stay in this browser. A slot's download is the Brick's own file: put slot1.txt to slot8.txt in Tools/tg5040/Spheres.pak/saves/ on the SD card, and the Brick's files open here too. Copy link puts the whole sky in a link.
