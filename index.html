About Spheres

Spheres is a generative instrument: a small solar system that plays music. At its centre the sun and three planets hold long, slowly shifting drones; around them three more planets play arpeggios. You set it going and shape it while you listen. Nothing has to be played note by note.

How it works

The drones. A droning planet's speed is its pitch. One set at 3:2 sounds a fifth above the sun, and circles three times while the sun's beam turns twice. Simple ratios like 3:2, 4:3 and 5:4 sound smooth and still. Gravity pulls the drones towards them; wander and comets push them away again, so the harmony breathes. The panel under the sky shows how each drone sits with the others: locked, gently beating, or rough.

The arps. Planets 4, 5 and 6 each play an arpeggio from a scale, in a meter of their own. Their notes sit round their circle and light up as they sound. Each can start over every few bars or with another planet, skip notes by chance, change a few notes as it goes, or move through the scale's chords.

The looper. Loop takes the bars just played and as many still to come, and plays them again and again, exactly: the same notes, chances and movements, not a recording. While it loops, only the Sound settings change. Press it again to carry on from there.

The tuning. Everything is tuned in just intervals above one root note. Move the root and the whole system moves with it.

Getting started

  1. Press Play (or Space) and listen for a while.
  2. Click a planet in the sky. Its column lights up, and the panel under the sky says what it is doing.
  3. Click a key on the piano under Root to move everything to another note.
  4. Try another Voice for a drone, or another Instrument or Scale for an arp.
  5. Turn up Gravity to hear the drones settle, or Wander to set them drifting.
  6. When you like what you hear, press Loop. File › Save keeps the sound for another day.

Changing values

  • Drag a value: right or up for more. Hold Shift to go finely.
  • Double-click a value to type it: 231 or Bb3 for the root, 3:2 for a speed.
  • With a value chosen, the arrow keys step it (Shift for big steps), and Delete puts back the value it started with.
  • On the sky, drag a drone up or down to change its speed. Let go near a simple ratio with the sun and it settles there; hold Alt to leave it free. Drag the sun to move the root, or an arp planet for shorter or longer notes. Double-click a planet to turn it on or off.
  • Ctrl Z undoes, Ctrl Shift Z redoes.
