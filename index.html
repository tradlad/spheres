DRONE SIM

Spheres

Drones at the centre, arpeggios circling them: a generative instrument made for the TrimUI Brick Pro handheld, running here as the same program in your browser.

Runs the same C code as the device build. The app draws the screen, and the engine plays in an audio worklet.

Try this

  1. Press START. At the centre the sun and three planets drone and orbit, as in the orrery. Round them, planets 4, 5 and 6 each play an arpeggio; its notes sit on its circle, coloured by scale degree, and light as they sound.
  2. Top left of the sky, the bar you are in (counted in one arp planet's meter) and its beats. MENU on any page loops: the bar you are in is the middle, and the bars up to its end and as many after it (4 and 4 to start with) play again and again, exactly: the notes and their dice, the orbits, comets, every setting and stick move. Not the sound, which rings on over each jump back, and the SOUND page stays yours. MENU again carries on from there. The LOOP page has the same button (A), the bars each side and the planet that counts.
  3. SELECT to DRONES: the sun and planets 1 to 3 (L1 / R1), their voice, speed (pitch and orbit together), orbit and moons, and the space they share: root, gravity, wander. Under Root, Note names it (equal tempered, A at 440 Hz); ◀ ▶ there jump the root from note to note, A puts it on the nearest. Everything over it stays in just intervals.
  4. SELECT to ARPS: planets 4 to 6 (L1 / R1). Each has its own instrument, scale (43 just scales, Balkan ones among them), notes (the scale or its chord), range, pattern, note length, meter, retrig and chance. A on Scale or Meter gives it to every arp planet.
  5. Retrig starts an arp over every 1, 2 or 4 bars, or makes it follow another arp planet: a new pass each time that one sounds a note. Retrig chance skips some start overs; Restart from begins each one at the first note, the note after last time's, or any note.
  6. CHANGE keeps a pass from simply repeating: Mutate changes some of its notes every so many bars (Mutate every), and the changes stay; Chords moves it to another chord of the scale every 2 to 16 bars, and home again. A planet that follows another takes its chord.
  7. On the sky, an arp planet's left stick steps its note length, X turns its metronome on (a click on each beat of its meter); R2 + right stick sets the tempo.
  8. SOUND has the drones' effects and the notes' effects apart: each its own filter, delay, reverb and level.
